The founder authorized up to ten new field passages per day on 2026-10-02. Later the same day, the founder explicitly authorized automatic approval of generated offering copy while in beta. Privacy protections remain.

**Why:** The earlier three-call limit was ambiguous because each call returned three passages. The review budget is passages, not batches. Alphabetical selection had concentrated coverage near the beginning of the alphabet.

**How to apply:** Count actual passages and conservatively account for older batch counters during transitions. Preserve approved content; an old purge instruction is not authorization to delete generated passages that have since been approved. Resistance detection terms need explicit approval independently of offering prose.

**Beta-only exception:** Generated offering passages may be published without individual editorial review during beta, including the previously pending generated drafts. This is not approval to activate generated resistance vocabulary, rewrite founder-selected prose, or autoapprove unrelated copy.

**Why:** The founder said to disable the approval gate for generated offerings “for now” and “approve everything while in beta.”

**How to apply:** Keep this exception reversible and scoped to offering generation. Turning it off restores review for future generations; do not retroactively revoke already approved passages without instruction.

Always verify deployed queue behavior before describing it as paused or active.

**Why:** A local pause guard was absent from the deployed function, so source-only diagnosis incorrectly said generation had stopped.

**How to apply:** Compare the live revision/source and scheduler when investigating queue behavior, rather than treating local code as proof of live state.
